Privacy policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

This policy describes how HVZEweb processes personal data of visitors to hvzeweb.github.io.

What data we collect

When you contact us via Telegram or email, you may share:

  • name;
  • email or Telegram;
  • message about your project.

Why we use the data

We use it only to respond to your request: discuss the project, estimate cost and answer questions.

How long we keep it

Data is kept until the conversation about your request is finished or until you ask us to delete it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share data with third parties, except technical services to deliver your message (Telegram, email).

Analytics

The site may use Yandex.Metrica for traffic analytics. The service collects anonymized visit data.

Your rights

You may request deletion or correction of your data by emailing hvzeweb@mail.ru.
